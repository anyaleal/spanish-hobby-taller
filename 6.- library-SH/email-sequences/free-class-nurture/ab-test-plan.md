# A/B test plan — subject lines

Test **one variable at a time**: the subject line. Keep preview text and body
constant so the read is clean. With a small early list, don't over-split — run
the highest-leverage tests first (Emails 1 and 4).

## Priority tests

| Email | Live subject (A) | Challenger (B) | Why test it |
|-------|------------------|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | What to do now that you've seen the map | The one thing a map of Spanish can't do | E1 sets open-rate baseline for the whole sequence. Curiosity vs. utility framing. |
| 4 | What working together actually looks like | The plainest description of my classes I can give you | The offer email. Small lift here moves bookings most. |
| 2 | Why "me gusta" has been tricking you | The verb that isn't the verb you think it is | Tests specific-hook vs. intrigue-hook on a value email. |

## How to read it
- **Primary metric:** unique open rate on the subject test itself.
- **What actually matters:** downstream **click-to-Calendly** and **bookings**.
  A subject that wins opens but loses clicks is the wrong winner. Judge on
  clicks/bookings, not opens alone.
- **Sample size:** with a small list, let a test run its full send before
  calling it. Don't declare a winner on a handful of opens.
