**FeatureCard** — icon-led feature tile; use for the homepage "what you'll explore" trio and similar concept groupings.

```jsx
<FeatureCard icon="fa-solid fa-eye-low-vision" title="The Hidden Structure">
  What gives Spanish its shape and logic — what you don't see, but is always there.
</FeatureCard>
<FeatureCard icon="fa-solid fa-puzzle-piece" title="The Building Blocks" highlighted>
  Learn what each piece does, how it behaves, and how they fit together.
</FeatureCard>
```

Teal circular icon badge, teal title, short accent rule, muted copy. Set `highlighted` for the peach featured middle tile (no shadow). Lay three across in a grid.
