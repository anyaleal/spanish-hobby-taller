# Companion stub — free → paid follow-up

Not part of the 7-email nurture. This is the *next* piece: what happens after
someone books and takes their two free classes, to convert them to the paid
$30 class. Stub only — flag for a future single-piece build.

## The gap it fills
The nurture's job ends at the first booking. Nothing yet carries a happy
free-trial student across to their first paid class. That handoff is where the
$30 revenue is won or lost.

## Rough shape (to build later, one piece, draft → review → ship)
1. **After class 1** — a short warm note: name one pattern that clicked for
   *them* specifically, and make booking class 2 frictionless (same Calendly).
2. **After class 2 (end of free trial)** — no hard sell. Reflect what changed in
   two classes, then the plain next step: classes are $30 from here, book when
   you want. Same open-door tone as Email 7.
3. **If they don't rebook within ~2 weeks** — one gentle check-in, then back to
   the newsletter. No pressure, ever.
