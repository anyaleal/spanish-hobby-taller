# Flow & escalation — Free-Class Nurture

## Entry
- **Trigger:** subscriber tagged as *Spanish Map downloader* (the lead-magnet
  form/tag). Wire the opt-in first — capture is still in preview mode.
- **One entry per subscriber.** Do not re-enter.

## Sequence (evergreen, no deadlines)

| # | Send day | Delay from previous | Angle | CTA |
|---|----------|---------------------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Day 0  | 0 days | The bridge (map → what a map can't do) | Book free class |
| 2 | Day 3  | 3 days | A pattern in action (*gustar*) | Book free class |
| 3 | Day 5  | 2 days | Why memorizing keeps failing | Book free class |
| 4 | Day 8  | 3 days | What the classes are (offer, price, 2 free) | Book free class |
| 5 | Day 11 | 3 days | The objection ("repackaged? / will I quit?") | Book free class |
| 6 | Day 14 | 3 days | A class, walked through | Book free class |
| 7 | Day 18 | 4 days | The warm close (standing invitation) | Book free class |

Send window: pick a single send hour (e.g. 9:00 in the subscriber's timezone).
All days allowed — no urgency logic, so no day restrictions needed.

## Exit / suppression
- **Booked a class** (Calendly): the goal is met. Suppress remaining emails.
  Best path: a Calendly→Kit automation (or Zapier) tags the subscriber
  *booked-class*; add that tag as a completion/removal condition on the sequence.
  Until that automation exists, the sequence keeps sending — harmless (all CTAs
  are the same open invitation), but ideally suppressed once booked.
- **Unsubscribed:** standard Kit suppression.
- **Purchased later ($30 class):** no change needed here; this sequence's job
  ends at the first booking.

## After the sequence
Subscriber stays on the newsletter (Email 7 P.S. sets that expectation). The
relationship continues via regular pattern broadcasts, not more sales pressure.
