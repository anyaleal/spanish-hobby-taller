**SectionBand** — full-bleed colored band to break up long pages and add contrast rhythm.

```jsx
<SectionBand tone="teal" rule icon="fa-solid fa-thumbs-up">
  <h2>All content on this site is 100% free and designed with care.</h2>
  <p>No paywalls, no sign-ups, no pressure — just a clear, thoughtful way to learn Spanish.</p>
</SectionBand>
```

Tones: `teal` (default, white text) · `brown` · `peach` (dark text). Headings inside inherit the band's text color. Use sparingly — one or two per page for contrast.
