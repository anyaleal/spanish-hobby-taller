## One-line positioning
Spanish Hobby helps English speakers discover how Spanish *actually works*, the
patterns and logic that connect the pieces, so learning it finally makes sense.
We don't help you memorize more. We help you memorize less, because you finally
understand. **Understanding is the heart. The human connection is the vehicle.**

## What we are / are not
- We ARE: an editorial education brand for curious adults. Knowledge, depth,
  discovery. Same shelf as The New York Times, Audible, MasterClass, Notion,
  National Geographic.
- We are NOT: a language app. No gamification, streaks, points, owls, or "fluent
  in 2 weeks." Tech and AI are tools in service of a human connection, never a
  replacement for it.

## The customer · "Sarah"
41, project manager at a tech company, Denver. Curious, well-read (novel + Kindle
+ idea notebook on the nightstand). Tried apps and courses before and always felt
she was *memorizing without understanding*. Loves Duolingo's accessibility,
Audible's smart-but-practical feel, and NYT for respecting her intelligence.

- **Keeps her up at night:** investing time but still not getting how the
  language really works; getting stuck at intermediate, translating in her head.
- **Brings her joy:** the click moment, one pattern that explains ten things she
  thought were unrelated. Solving a small mystery.
- **Her objection before buying:** "Is this actually different, or the same
  information repackaged? Will I quit like last time?" She's not afraid of
  effort, she's afraid of feeling lost again after spending it.

**Who we do NOT sell to:** shortcut-seekers, vacation-phrase memorizers, streak
chasers, anyone who treats learning as an obligation rather than curiosity.

## Voice
- Tone axes (from the Brief): **70% close / 30% formal**, **serious** (not jokey),
  **classic** (not disruptive), **technical** (explains mechanics, not just feel-good).
- Archetype: **Sage** (dominant) + **Explorer** (secondary). The root verb is
  **discover**. We don't just know, we invite you to explore with us.
- The party-person test: in a small group, asks genuine questions, explains
  complex ideas so they feel obvious, never shows off. People leave thinking
  "that was interesting," not "how smart she is."
- **Emotional consigna** (the feeling every first impression should trigger):
  curiosity + relief, *"Ah... I think I'm actually going to get this."* Not
  excitement, not urgency, not entertainment.

## Say this / never say this
- SAY: "here's *why* it works this way," "notice the pattern," "this one idea
  explains ten things," "you'll memorize less, not more."
- NEVER: "easy/fast/hack," "learn X words in Y days," "don't think, just
  practice," anything that treats Spanish as a pile of facts to cram, hype,
  fake urgency, or fabricated results.
- **Never use em-dashes or en-dashes, ever** (regla permanente de Anya,
  14 ago 2026). Use the punctuation that fits each sentence's rhythm: comma,
  period, semicolon, colon, parentheses. Chosen by rhythm, not one by default.
  (Replaces the old "em-dashes closed" rule.)
- **Spanish quoted inside English copy is always marked** (regla permanente de
  Anya, 15 ago 2026). On the web, real italics. On social, quotation marks,
  because social platforms are plain text. Never Unicode fake italics: they break
  screen readers and search indexing. Detail in `seo-social.md`.
- **The offer is two free classes, never one** (regla permanente de Anya,
  15 ago 2026). Write it as "book your two free classes."
- Mark any unverified stat/quote/result [PLACEHOLDER].

## Reference brands (why the customer loves them)
Notion (clarity, white space), MasterClass (authority + warmth), NYT (editorial
respect), Audible (smart & practical), Airbnb (exploration + human warmth),
Bible.com/YouVersion (treats the reader as a serious adult). Common thread:
they treat the user as a **reader, not a consumer**.

## Visual shorthand (for when copy meets design)
Calm/trust palette: deep blues, muted teals/petrol with a warm accent. No neons,
no Duolingo green, no red+yellow flag clichés, no all-black luxury. Humanist
sans (Inter/Söhne) for digital, a serif (Source Serif/Charter) for editorial
moments. Free Google Fonts only (bootstrap).
