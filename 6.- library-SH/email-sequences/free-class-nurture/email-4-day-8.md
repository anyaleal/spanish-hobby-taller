Over the last few emails I've shown you a pattern hiding in plain sight, and why the intermediate plateau happens in the first place. Now let me describe, as plainly as I can, what actually working together looks like, so you can decide with clear eyes.

It's one to one, live, just you and me on a call. There's no fixed syllabus you have to keep pace with, because the whole class is built around your Spanish, the sentences you're reaching for and the ones that keep tripping you, so you bring what's confusing you, we find the pattern sitting underneath it, and you leave understanding something you used to only memorize. That's the entire shape of it.

You schedule around your own life, not mine. There's no cohort to join and no start date to miss, you just pick a time that fits your week, and when you want another class, you book another.

Now the honest part, the money. Each class is thirty dollars, and the first two are free, genuinely free, no card and no auto-charge waiting to catch you. Two rather than one because a single class is a taste, while two is enough to actually test whether this way of learning fits the way your mind works. If after those two it isn't for you, you'll have spent nothing but an hour of curiosity.

This isn't for someone hunting a shortcut to fluency by Friday. It's for the person who would rather understand ten things through one idea than memorize ten things separately, and if that's you, the next step is a small one.
